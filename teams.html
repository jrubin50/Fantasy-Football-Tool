Offensive efficiency (EPA) and pace of play for all 32 teams, 2025 regular season

2025 REG SEASON
Offensive EPA/play (Expected Points Added per play) measures how many points a team's offense is worth versus expectation on the average snap — the single best public measure of offensive efficiency. A team with a high, positive EPA/play is scoring more than expected on a typical play, which means more touchdowns and more fantasy points to go around for its skill players.

Plays/game (pace) is offensive snap volume — how many real offensive plays (pass + rush, excluding kneels/spikes) a team runs per game. This isn't inherently good or bad like EPA, but it matters for fantasy: a fast-paced offense creates more total opportunities (targets, carries) even if it isn't hyper-efficient, which raises the floor for its skill players. Sourced from nflverse play-by-play data — not from the live Sleeper API this tool otherwise uses, since Sleeper doesn't expose play-by-play or EPA.
